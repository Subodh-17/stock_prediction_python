RMSE / MAE / R² are PRICE ERROR metrics (how close the predicted dollar price was). "Directional Accuracy" is a separate, unrelated metric -- the % of test-set days the model correctly called whether price would go up or down. A model can have good directional accuracy with mediocre price error, or vice versa; neither number is "the" accuracy of the model on its own, and this is a backward-looking, held-out-test-set measurement, not a guarantee about future predictions.
